## RISK MANAGEMENT

### RISK 1: EMPLOYEE CYNICISM (Likelihood: HIGH, Impact: CRITICAL) - BIGGEST FEAR

**Early Warning Signals:**
- Pulse survey Week 6: <50% say "Recognition feels genuine"
- Sarcastic comments in team meetings ("Oh great, mandatory compliments")
- Eye-rolling or disengagement when recognition given publicly
- High opt-out rate from public recognition (>30%)

**Proactive Mitigation:**
1. **Transparency:** Share full logic model, evidence (HR=0.72), and WHY weekly recognition matters in Week 2 kickoff
2. **Individualized Training:** Hour 2 of manager training = practice distinguishing specific ("Sarah, your client presentation Tuesday was excellent—the way you handled the pricing objection showed deep product knowledge") vs. generic ("Good job, team!")
3. **Employee Control:** Voluntary public recognition (opt-out option reduces defensiveness)
4. **CEO Modeling:** CEO recognizes 2-3 employees Week 2 BEFORE managers cascade

**Reactive Mitigation:**
- Week 6: If <50% say genuine → Emergency town hall ("What would make this feel more authentic?")
- Week 9: If still <50% → Pivot to employee-designed approach
- Week 12: If cynicism persists → STOP program and conduct post-mortem

### RISK 2: MANAGER NON-COMPLIANCE (Likelihood: MODERATE, Impact: HIGH)

**Early Warning Signals:**
- Google Sheets Week 4: <3 recognition entries per manager (should be ~10)
- Employee survey Week 6: Recognition still at 23% baseline (not moving toward 65% target)
- Manager excuses: "I forgot," "Too busy," "My team knows I appreciate them"

**Proactive Mitigation:**
1. **Auto-Reminders:** Google Sheets sends weekly Friday prompt "Have you recognized your team this week?"
2. **Peer Accountability:** Five managers meet monthly (Week 4, 8, 12) to share examples
3. **CEO Expectation:** CEO attends first 15 min of training and says "This is a priority. Weekly recognition is part of your management responsibilities."

**Reactive Mitigation:**
- Week 4: If any manager <3 entries → HR 1-on-1 ("What's the barrier?")
- Week 8: If still non-compliant → CEO meets with manager
- Week 12: If 2+ of 5 managers non-compliant → Program cannot succeed

### RISK 3: NO ENGAGEMENT CHANGE (Likelihood: LOW, Impact: HIGH)

**Early Warning Signals:**
- Week 6 pulse survey: Engagement unchanged (still 62%)
- Week 9 pulse survey: Engagement declining
- Employee comments: "Recognition is nice, but doesn't fix [underlying issue]"

**Proactive Mitigation:**
1. **Root Cause Alignment:** Target lowest organizational scores (autonomy 58%, development 64%) not just generic recognition
2. **Realistic Expectations:** Clarify "We expect small improvements (2-4 points) in 12 weeks, larger improvements (8 points) in 6 months"

**Reactive Mitigation:**
- Week 6: If no change → Investigate (Are employees receiving but not valuing? Different root cause like workload/burnout?)
- Week 9: If still no change → Acknowledge and pivot ("Recognition is great, but we're burned out from 60-hour weeks - let's address workload first")
- Week 13: If <64% despite full implementation → Honest recommendation: "Recognition is necessary but insufficient for this organization's engagement challenges. MAINTAIN practice + GATHER MORE DATA on true root causes."

## DECISION RULES (Week 14 Evaluation)

### CONTINUE (Engagement ≥67%)
- Evidence working: Recognition + autonomy/growth interventions driving engagement
- Recommendation: Expand to 6-month program, set new target (70% → 75%), measure turnover at 6-month mark
- Investment: Maintain manager time (1.7 hours/week), HR monitoring (2 hours/week), continue pulse surveys

### ADJUST (Engagement 64-66%)
- Partial effect: Some improvement but below target
- Investigation Needed:
  - Manager non-compliance? (Check Google Sheets: Are all 5 managers logging >40 entries?)
  - Employee cynicism? (Check authenticity scores: Is <70% saying recognition genuine?)
  - Wrong interventions? (Check autonomy/development scores: Did they improve?)
- Recommendation: Address specific barrier identified, extend pilot 4-8 weeks with adjustments

### PIVOT (Engagement <64%)
- Approach not working: Minimal or no improvement despite faithful implementation
- Investigation Needed:
  - Is recognition the right intervention? (Employee feedback: "Recognition is nice, but doesn't fix [X]")
  - Is there a different root cause? (Workload, leadership, client stress, compensation below market)
- Recommendation:
  - If different root cause identified: Shift focus to that issue
  - If unclear root cause: GATHER MORE DATA (employee focus groups, exit interviews)
  - Maintain recognition practices (good management hygiene) but don't expect major engagement gains
