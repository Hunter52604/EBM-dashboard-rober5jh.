• 62% engagement (4.2 points below benchmark)
• 18% turnover (5 points above normal)
• 23% get weekly recognition (10 points below standard)
• Pay freeze - can't use salary increases

**INPUTS:**
• 5 managers × 2hr training
• HR: 62 hours over 16 weeks
• Budget: $1,000
• Google Sheets tracker

**ACTIVITIES:**
1. Train managers on individualized recognition (Week 1-2)
2. Weekly public recognition (team meetings, 5 min)
3. Weekly private recognition (1-on-1s, 5 min)
4. Quarterly growth conversations (1 hour each)

**OUTPUTS:**
• 5 trained managers
• 65% weekly recognition coverage (up from 23%)
• 600+ logged recognition entries
• 50 development conversations

**OUTCOMES (12 weeks):**
• Engagement: 62% → 70% (+8 points, 95% confidence)
• Recognition: 23% → 65% (+42 points, 95% confidence)
• Autonomy: 58% → 66% (+8 points, 88% confidence)
• Development: 64% → 72% (+8 points, 88% confidence)

**IMPACT (6-12 months):**
• Turnover: 18% → 13% (-5 points, 75% confidence)
• Cost savings: $187,500/year
• Competitive advantage in non-monetary engagement

### PHASE 1: LAUNCH (Q1 2026, Weeks 1-2)

**1. Manager Training (Week 1-2)**
- 2-hour sessions for each of 5 managers (individual scheduling for flexibility)
- Content: Individualized recognition (2.2X more effective per research β=0.41), practice scenarios, Google Sheets
- Success: 100% attendance, >80% confidence, distinguish specific vs. generic
- Time: HR 10hrs, managers 10hrs total
- Budget: $300-400

**2. Soft Launch (Week 2)**
- 2-3 champion managers start first
- Test system, refine approach, gauge employee response
- Success: 5+ Google Sheets entries, no cynicism signals, CEO models at all-hands
- Time: Champions 1hr each, HR 2hrs, CEO 15min

**3. Kickoff Communication (Week 2)**
- All-hands meeting (30 min) + follow-up email
- Explain evidence (HR=0.72 effect size), logic model, voluntary nature
- Success: >80% attendance, <5 opt-outs, >60% understand purpose
- Time: HR 5hrs, CEO 30min, employees 30min

**Launch Milestones:**
✓ All 5 managers trained with >80% confidence
✓ Soft launch successful with positive feedback
✓ Kickoff meeting >80% attendance
✓ Google Sheets functional with 5-10 entries

### PHASE 2: FULL IMPLEMENTATION (Weeks 3-14, 12-week pilot)

**1. Weekly Recognition System**
- **What:** All 5 managers: public (team meetings) + private (1-on-1s) recognition
- **Target:** 600+ Google Sheets entries, 65% coverage, each person recognized 8+ of 12 weeks
- **Time:** Managers 30min/week, HR 2hrs/week
- **Checkpoints:**
  - Week 4: All managers >10 entries
  - Week 8: >50% weekly recognition
  - Week 14: 390+ total entries

**2. Autonomy & Growth Integration**
- **What:** 50 development conversations (1hr each), 2-3 autonomy experiments
- **Focus:** Lowest FEVS scores (autonomy 58%, development 64%)
- **Target:** All conversations by Week 8, scores improve 4+ points
- **Time:** Managers 50hrs total, HR 3hrs

**3. Cynicism Monitoring**
- **What:** Monthly pulse surveys (Week 6, 9, 12), manager retrospectives
- **Key:** "Recognition feels genuine" (1-5 scale)
- **Target:** >70% say genuine by Week 12
- **Escalation:** <50% triggers CEO town hall within 3 days
- **Time:** HR 6hrs, employees 15min total, managers 3hrs

**Implementation Milestones:**
✓ Week 4: All managers >50 combined entries
✓ Week 8: 50 conversations done, >50% weekly recognition
✓ Week 12: >70% say recognition feels genuine
✓ Week 14: 390+ entries (65% coverage)

**High-Priority Risks & Mitigation:**

1. **Manager Non-Compliance**
   - Monitor: Weekly Google Sheets review
   - Intervene: Week 4 if <10 entries (HR coaching)
   - Escalate: Week 8 if still non-compliant (CEO meeting)

2. **Employee Cynicism**
   - Monitor: Monthly pulse surveys
   - Intervene: <50% genuine triggers town hall within 3 days
   - Pivot: Employee task force redesigns approach if persistent

3. **Time Constraints**
   - Streamline: 30-min conversations if 1-hour not feasible
   - Integrate: Use existing meetings where possible
   - Prioritize: Focus on highest-impact activities first

4. **Timeline Mismatch**
   - Clarify: 12 weeks measures adoption, not outcomes
   - Track: Leading indicators (recognition frequency, engagement)
   - Plan: 6-12 month follow-up for turnover impact

**Key Assumptions:**
• Managers follow through consistently (not "checking box")
• Recognition feels genuine to employees
• Weekly frequency maintained
• Opportunities for autonomy exist in current roles
