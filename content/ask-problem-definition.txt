## Business Case and Impact Analysis

**Financial Impact Quantification - Converting Turnover Costs to Engagement Investment:**

This financial diagram shows that non-monetary interventions deliver measurable ROI.
A $2,000 per manager operational budget generates $675K savings through turnover reduction,
helping leadership see engagement improvement as a COST REDUCTION STRATEGY, not an expense.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    BASELINE → TARGET ROI TABLE                              │
├─────────────────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┬─────────────────────┤
│ METRIC                  │   BASELINE   │    TARGET    │      SAVINGS        │
├─────────────────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┼─────────────────────┤
│ Turnover Rate           │     18%      │     13%      │   -5 points         │
│ Departures (300 empl)   │   54 people  │   39 people  │   -15 people        │
│ Cost/Departure (SHRM)   │   $45,000    │   $45,000    │   (industry avg)    │
├─────────────────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┼─────────────────────┤
│ Annual Turnover Cost    │ $2,430,000   │ $1,755,000   │   $675,000 Year 1   │
│ Two-Year Cumulative     │              │              │   $1,350,000        │
└─────────────────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┴─────────────────────┘

• Source: SHRM (industry standard) reports a range of ~$15,000–$75,000 cost per voluntary
  departure depending on role level (2024). Using $45,000 midpoint as baseline.
• Current turnover cost: 18% × 300 employees = 54 departures × $45,000 = $2,430,000 annually
• Target turnover cost: 13% × 300 employees = 39 departures × $45,000 = $1,755,000 annually
• Estimated first-year savings (difference): $2,430,000 − $1,755,000 = $675,000
• Estimated two-year savings (straight line): $675,000 × 2 = $1,350,000
