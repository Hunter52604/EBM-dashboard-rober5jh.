**2. Voluntary Turnover** — [SPECIFIC & TIME-BOUND]
   • Baseline: 18% annualized (9 of 50 employees, $337,500 annual cost)
   • Target: 13% (-5 points = 28% reduction, HR=0.72 from field study N=10,800)
   • Financial Impact: $187,500/year savings IF achieved (4.5 fewer departures × $37,500 cost)
   • Timeline: 18-24 MONTHS required (NOT measurable in 12-week pilot)
   • Method: Track via HRIS, measure at Month 12/18/24 post-pilot
   • Evidence basis: HR=0.72 (weekly recognition → 28% turnover reduction over 18-24 months)
   • Note: Pilot measures LEADING indicators (engagement, recognition coverage, authenticity)
     that predict whether turnover reduction will likely follow

**3. Recognition Coverage** — [ACHIEVABLE & MEASURABLE]

   • Target: 65% (+42 points, HR=0.72 threshold for turnover reduction)
   • Pilot Target: ≥50% minimum, ≥55% strong success, ≥65% full target
   • Method: Google Sheets tracking (target 600+ entries over 12 weeks = 65% coverage)
   • Timeline: Weekly tracking, Week 13 final count
   • Evidence basis: Gallup 350M+ surveys show 33% benchmark, HR=0.72 requires weekly frequency

## IMPLEMENTATION INDICATORS (Adoption) — Are people using interventions?

These show whether the intervention is being implemented faithfully. Both necessary for success.

**1. Manager Training Completion** — [MEASURABLE]
   • Baseline: 0% (no formal recognition training currently)
   • Target: 100% of 5 managers complete 2-hour training with >80% confidence post-training
   • Timeline: Week 1-2
   • Method: Post-training survey, confidence rating 1-10 scale
   • Decision trigger: <80% confidence → Additional coaching required

**2. Manager Implementation Fidelity** — [SPECIFIC & RELEVANT]
   • Baseline: Current ad-hoc recognition (no tracking)
   • Target: 80%+ of managers (4 of 5) achieve >40 Google Sheets entries over 12 weeks
   • Method: Google Sheets audit every Monday, cumulative count
   • Week 4 checkpoint: <10 entries → HR coaching intervention
   • Week 8 checkpoint: <25 entries → CEO escalation
   • Evidence: β=0.41 individualized recognition requires manager capability

**3. Recognition Authenticity** — [CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTOR]
   • Target: >70% employees perceive recognition as genuine (addresses biggest fear: cynicism)
   • Threshold: <50% authentic triggers emergency town hall
   • Method: Pulse surveys Week 6/9/12, 1 question: "Recognition feels genuine (not forced)"
   • Evidence: Addresses stakeholder evidence (all 5 groups support IF authentic)

**4. Development Conversations** — [TIME-BOUND]
   • Target: 100% of 50 employees complete development conversation by Week 8
   • Method: Manager self-report, HR verification via spot-checks
   • Decision trigger: Non-compliant managers → CEO escalation
   • Evidence: β=0.29-0.33 autonomy/growth effect requires development focus

**5. Survey Response Rate** — [DATA QUALITY]
   • Target: >80% baseline/post-pilot, >60% pulse surveys
   • Method: Track responses vs. 50-employee roster
   • Decision trigger: <60% → Data quality concern, may need to extend timeline
   • Critical: Representative data needed for valid Continue/Adjust/Pivot decision

## Early Warning Signals (Week 1-6) — Triggers for Immediate Action

POSITIVE SIGNALS (Continue as planned):
• Manager training confidence >80% (Week 2)
• Google Sheets entries >30 in first 4 weeks (Week 4 checkpoint met)
• Employee questions about program (curiosity indicates engagement)
• Pulse survey authenticity >60% (Week 6)
• No time burden complaints from managers

RED FLAGS (Triggers immediate intervention):
• <50% authenticity Week 6 → Emergency town hall (address cynicism immediately)
• <10 Google Sheets entries Week 4 → HR coaching for non-compliant manager
• Manager reports >2 hours/week → Streamline processes, reduce admin burden
• Survey response <60% → Investigate participation barriers, extend deadline
• Negative open-ended themes → Focus groups to understand root cause

## Must-Achieve Criteria (ALL Required for Success)

1. **Manager Implementation Fidelity:** 80%+ (4 of 5 managers) achieve >40 Google Sheets entries
2. **Recognition Coverage Threshold:** ≥50% employees receive weekly recognition (up from 23%)
3. **Authenticity Threshold:** ≥60% perceive recognition as genuine (addresses cynicism fear)
4. **Engagement Directional Improvement:** ≥64% (+2 minimum shows progress toward 70% target)
5. **Survey Data Quality:** ≥70% response rate (ensures representative data)

**Decision:** If ANY Must-Achieve criterion fails → PIVOT (fundamental implementation problem)

---

## Continue Decision (Strong Success Profile)

**Quantitative Indicators:**
- Engagement ≥67% (+5 to +8 points from 62% baseline)
- Recognition coverage ≥55% (600-700 Google Sheets entries)
- Authenticity >70% (strong majority perceive genuine)
- Manager compliance 80-100% (4-5 of 5 managers)
- Autonomy/Development +4 to +8 points each

**Qualitative Indicators:**
- Open-ended themes cite recognition positively ("My manager notices my work")
- Google Sheets >70% high-quality entries (specific, individualized, behavior-focused)
- Manager retrospectives positive (sustainable, valuable, seeing results)
- No major unintended negative consequences

**Action:** 6-month extension, 70%→75% stretch target, measure turnover 12-18 months,
institutionalize (job descriptions, performance reviews, onboarding)

---

## Adjust Decision (Moderate Success Profile)

**Quantitative Indicators:**
- Engagement 64-66% (+2 to +4 points, working but needs optimization)
- Recognition coverage 40-54% (430-580 entries, partial adoption)
- Authenticity 50-70% (mixed perceptions, some cynicism remains)
- Manager compliance 60-80% (3 of 5 managers, gaps identified)
- Autonomy/Development +0 to +3 points (limited progress)

**Qualitative Indicators:**
- Mixed themes (recognition helpful BUT workload/pay concerns remain)
- Google Sheets 50-70% quality (generic recognition common, needs coaching)
- Manager retrospectives identify barriers (time pressure, unclear how to individualize)

**Action:** Investigate barriers via manager interviews + employee focus groups, implement
targeted adjustments (if non-compliance → CEO accountability, if authenticity → more private
recognition, if workload → workload reduction pilot, if timeline → extend 4-8 weeks),
re-evaluate with clear go/no-go criteria

---

## Pivot Decision (Weak Success Profile)

**Quantitative Indicators:**
- Engagement <64% (0 to +1 points, insufficient progress)
- Recognition coverage <40% (<430 entries, implementation failure)
- Authenticity <50% (cynicism fear realized, fake/forced perception dominant)
- Manager compliance <60% (0-2 of 5 managers, scaling not viable)
- Autonomy/Development flat or negative (no mechanism working)

**Qualitative Indicators:**
- Themes NOT recognition-related (workload, pay, leadership issues dominate)
- Google Sheets <50% quality (checkbox compliance, not authentic)
- Manager retrospectives report deflection, unsustainability, value questioned
- Negative unintended consequences (employee discomfort, favoritism perceptions)

**Action - Three Options:**
1. **Different intervention** if different root cause identified (e.g., workload reduction,
   compensation study, leadership coaching), maintain recognition as hygiene factor
2. **Honest assessment** if root cause unclear, gather more data via focus groups, exit
   interviews, stay interviews before next intervention
3. **Evidence doesn't apply** if faithful implementation produced no results (50-person
   context different from 500-5,000 employee evidence base, professional services unique,
   salary freeze creates different dynamic)

## Evidence-Based Go/No Criteria:

Continue IF (All conditions must be met):

   [Engagement +4 points OR Productivity +3%]
            AND
   [Manager usefulness ≥7/10]
            AND
   [No red flags detected]
            AND
   [All improvements achieved with ZERO salary budget]

DO NOT Continue IF:
• Success metrics not met
• Manager time burden excessive (>2 hours/week)
• Negative employee feedback about "doing more with less"
• Low adoption rates (<50% participation)

MODIFY & RE-TEST IF:
• Partial success (one criterion met but not all)
• Promising early indicators but other challenges
• Manager feedback suggests design improvements

This demonstrates commitment to data-driven decisions even if results disappoint.
Pilot risks organizational change by enabling course correction before full investment.
