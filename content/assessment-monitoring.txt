#### Secondary Outcome KPIs

- Baseline: 23% | Target: 65% (+42 points, HR=0.72)
- Pulse surveys Week 6, 9, 12 + Google Sheets validation (600+ entries)
- Rules: <40% Week 6 → investigate non-compliance | 40-55% Week 12 → extend 4-8 weeks | ≥65% → full target

**KPI 2: Authenticity** (Addresses Cynicism Risk)
- Baseline: ~50% skeptical | Target: >70% "recognition feels genuine"
- Pulse surveys monthly (1-5 scale, % saying 4-5)
- Rules: <50% → EMERGENCY town hall within 3 days | 50-70% → monitor/coach | >70% → cynicism addressed

**KPI 3: Autonomy**
- Baseline: 58% | Target: 66% (+8 points, β=0.33)
- Pre/post survey Week -3 and 13
- Rules: <62% → insufficient autonomy | 62-65% → directional | ≥66% → full target

**KPI 4: Development**
- Baseline: 64% | Target: 72% (+8 points, β=0.29)
- Pre/post survey Week -3 and 13
- Rules: <68% → investigate quality | 68-71% → directional | ≥72% → full target

**KPI 5: Intent to Leave** (Leading Indicator, NOT actual turnover in 12 weeks)
- Baseline: Unknown | Target: 10-15% reduction
- Pre/post survey "considering leaving in next 12 months" (Yes/No)
- Note: Evidence shows 18-24 months for turnover effects. 12-week pilot measures intent, not actual 18% → 13% turnover reduction.

### Process KPIs (How Implementation Went - Did We Execute the Program Faithfully?)

#### Implementation Quality KPIs

**KPI 6: Manager Training Completion**
- Target: 100% by Week 2, >80% confidence post-training
- HR attendance + post-training survey
- Rules: <100% → delay launch, mandatory makeup within 3 days

**KPI 7: Manager Implementation Compliance**
- Target: 100% managers log >40 entries (Week 4 ~13, Week 8 ~27, Week 12 ~40)
- Google Sheets audit every Monday
- Rules: Week 4 <10 → HR coaching | Week 8 <20 → CEO escalation | Week 12 <30 (2+ managers) → program fails, Continue with compliant only or Pivot

**KPI 8: Development Conversations**
- Target: 100% of 50 employees by Week 8
- Google Sheets tracking (manager logs: employee, date, themes)
- Rules: <80% Week 8 → extend to Week 10 OR offer 30-min alternative

**KPI 9: Survey Response Rates** (Data Quality)
- Target: >80% baseline/post-pilot, >60% pulse surveys
- Google Forms count, reminders at 24hr and 48hr
- Rules: <70% → caveat "may not represent all views"

#### Stakeholder Engagement KPIs

**KPI 10: CEO Visible Support**
- Target: Attends training 15 min, models recognition Week 2, reads updates, attends Week 14 decision meeting
- HR checklist: Y/N for each activity
- Rules: If CEO disengages → escalate "visible support critical, re-confirm commitment or pause?"

**KPI 11: Manager Satisfaction**
- Target: >70% say program valuable and sustainable
- Week 14 anonymous survey (5 questions: helpful, sustainable, recommend, confident, continue)
- Rules: <70% → investigate concerns before continuing

**KPI 12: Employee Satisfaction**
- Target: >65% want program to continue
- Week 13 survey "want to continue?" (Yes/No/Unsure)
- Rules: <65% despite engagement increase → investigate disconnect

#### Resource Utilization KPIs

**KPI 13: Budget**
- Target: ≤$1,000 (Training $400, Materials $100, Surveys $200, Comms $200, Contingency $200)
- HR logs weekly, shares with CEO
- Rules: Approaching $900 → identify cuts before exceeding

**KPI 14: Time Investment**
- Target: Managers ≤1.7 hrs/week, HR ≤4 hrs/week
- Self-reported logs + retrospectives Week 4, 8, 12
- Rules: >2.5 hrs/week → unsustainable, simplify program

### Impact KPIs (Broader Organizational Effects Beyond Direct Program Targets)

#### Organizational Performance KPIs

**KPI 15: Client Satisfaction** (Unintended Consequence Check)
- Baseline: Q4 2025 NPS/CSAT | Target: Stable or improved (no decline >5 points)
- CRM data Q1 2026 vs Q4 2025
- Rules: >5 point decline → investigate if program distracted from client work

**KPI 16: Actual Turnover** (Long-term, NOT in 12-Week Pilot)
- Baseline: 18% | Target: 13% (-5 points, 75% confidence, requires 18-24 months)
- HRIS monthly tracking, follow-ups at 6, 12, 18 months
- Note: 12-week pilot CANNOT measure this. If CEO asks Week 14 → emphasize "need 6-12 month follow-up"

#### Cultural KPIs

**KPI 17: Recognition Culture** (Spillover Effect)
- Target: Spontaneous peer-to-peer recognition emerges
- Google Sheets "Recognize a Peer" tab + survey themes
- Rules: 5+ peer entries OR 10+ employees mention → strong cultural shift | None → limited to manager-employee only

## Data Collection Plan

### Quantitative Data Collection

#### Organizational Data
**Turnover:** HRIS monthly (first Monday) → calculate annualized rate. Note: 12-week pilot won't show reduction (need 18-24 months).

**Recognition Frequency:** Google Sheets continuous logging, HR audits every Monday → count by manager (>40 target),
 by employee (65% coverage), weekly trend (ramp-up vs decline), public vs private ratio.

**Client Satisfaction:** CRM quarterly (Q4 2025 baseline vs Q1 2026) → simple difference, >5 point decline = investigate.

#### Survey Data
**Survey 1: Baseline (Week -3)** - 50 employees, >80% response, 72-hour window
- 10-question engagement index (expect 62%)
- Recognition frequency (expect 23%), autonomy (58%), development (64%), intent to leave
- Recognition preferences (public/private/both/opt-out)
- Open-ended: "What would increase engagement?"
- Google Forms, two reminders at 24hr and 48hr

**Survey 2: Pulse (Week 6, 9, 12)** - 50 employees, >60% response
- Recognition frequency (track toward 65%)
- Authenticity (target >70%, <50% triggers emergency)
- 3-question engagement snapshot
- Open-ended: "What's working/not working?"

**Survey 3: Post-Pilot (Week 13)** - 50 employees, >80% response
- SAME 10-question index as baseline for comparison
- Program continuation ("Want to continue?" >65% target)
- Program impact ("Made me feel more valued")
- Open-ended: "What worked well?" "What should change?"

**Survey 4: Manager Satisfaction (Week 14)** - 5 managers, 100% response
- 5 questions: helped me, sustainable workload, confident, recommend, participate in extension
- Open-ended: barriers, support needed, changes
- Anonymous Google Forms

### Qualitative Data Collection

**Manager Coaching** (As-needed, Weeks 4-12): 30-60 min sessions with non-compliant managers (<10 entries Week 4, <20 Week 8).
 Topics: barriers (time, skill, belief), employee response, support needed. Document themes for CEO reporting.

**Employee Focus Groups** (Optional, Week 6/9 if <50% authenticity): 5-7 volunteers, 45-60 min.
 Topics: genuine vs fake recognition, how to improve authenticity, is recognition addressing right issue? CEO or HR facilitates.

**Pre-Launch Focus Group** (Optional, Week -2): 5-7 volunteers test messaging, preferences, trust-building.

**Team Meeting Observations** (Optional spot-checks, Week 4/8/12): HR attends 1-2 team meetings, documents recognition quality (specific vs generic),
 employee response, time spent (target 5 min). Compare to Google Sheets entries.

**Google Sheets Content Analysis** (Weeks 3-14): HR samples 25-50 entries weekly, codes for quality: Specific vs Generic, Individualized vs Template,
 Behavior-focused vs Outcome-only. >70% high-quality = training effective | <50% = compliance without authenticity, triggers β=0.41 coaching.

**Manager Retrospectives** (Week 4, 8, 12): 1 hour, HR facilitates, documents successes, barriers, solutions, peer advice. Code themes across 3 sessions,
 identify patterns (chronic vs resolved issues).

## Data Analysis Plan

### Quantitative Analysis
**Engagement Change:** Post-pilot % MINUS baseline % = change score (70%-62%=+8 full target | 67%-62%=+5 Continue | 63%-62%=+1 Pivot)
- No control group = cannot prove causation, HIGH interpretation bias
- Mitigation: Qualitative triangulation (do employees cite recognition?)
- Practical significance: +8=strong | +5-7=moderate | +2-4=small | 0-1=none | negative=backfired
- Context: 8 points in 12 weeks ambitious (most programs 2-4 points/year), 50% confidence on timeline

**Secondary Outcomes:** Simple difference scores (recognition 23%→65%, autonomy 58%→66%, development 64%→72%, intent to leave reduction)
- Cross-validate recognition with Google Sheets (600 entries ÷ 50 ÷ 12 weeks = ~1/employee/week = 65% coverage)

**Trend Analysis:**
- Google Sheets weekly: Ramp-up (sustainable) vs Decline (unsustainable) vs Volatile (inconsistent)
- Pulse surveys: Improving (momentum) vs Flat (plateau) vs Declining (losing effectiveness)

### Qualitative Analysis
**Survey Coding:** Export responses → develop code list → apply codes → count frequency → identify dominant themes (>20% = significant)
- Recognition themes strengthen causation | Workload/pay themes suggest wrong intervention

**Google Sheets Quality:** Sample 100-150 entries, code Specific (1/0) + Individualized (1/0) + Behavior-focused (1/0), calculate % high-quality (score 2-3)
- >70% = training effective (β=0.41) | 50-70% = mixed | <50% = compliance without authenticity (β=0.19)

### Mixed Methods Triangulation
**STRONG SUCCESS:** Quant targets met + qual confirms recognition + quality >70% + managers satisfied → CONTINUE
**MODERATE SUCCESS:** Partial quant + mixed qual + quality 50-70% + barriers identified → ADJUST
**WEAK/NO SUCCESS:** Minimal quant + non-recognition themes + quality <50% + cynicism → PIVOT

2. **Recognition Coverage:** ≥50% employees weekly recognition by Week 12
3. **Authenticity:** ≥60% say genuine by Week 12 (addresses cynicism)
4. **Engagement Direction:** ≥64% by Week 13 (+2 points minimum)
5. **Data Quality:** ≥70% response rate baseline and post-pilot

1. **Full Engagement:** 70% (+8 points) - 95% confidence target
2. **Full Recognition:** 65% weekly (+42 points) - HR=0.72 threshold
3. **Full Authenticity:** >70% genuine - overcomes cynicism
4. **Autonomy/Development:** Both +8 points - β=0.29-0.33 drivers
5. **100% Manager Compliance:** All 5 managers >40 entries - scalability
