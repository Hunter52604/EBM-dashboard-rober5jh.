### Evidence Type 2: PRACTITIONER (Gallup + SHRM)

**Evidence Strength:** MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Practice Base:** 350M+ surveys (Gallup), 340K+ HR professionals (SHRM)
- **Experience:** 80+ years (Gallup), 75+ years (SHRM)
- **Relevance:** Mid-market focus, non-monetary emphasis

**Key Findings:**
   - Only 33% get weekly recognition (our 23% is BELOW benchmark by 10 points)
   - 79% quit cite "lack of appreciation" - recognition addresses #1 turnover driver
   - Only 12% train managers on recognition - **we have competitive advantage here**
   - Zero-budget gets 70% of benefits (validates salary-freeze constraint)
   - +8-12 points engagement realistic (matches our 62%→70% = +8 target)
   - 15-31% turnover reduction possible (our 28% is mid-range)
   - ROI: $4.25 return per $1 spent

**Likelihood Ratio:**
- **Problem Validation:** 2.5X increase (60% → 85% posterior)
- **Recognition Works:** 2.0X increase (88% → 95% posterior - reinforces scientific)
- **Manager Training Gap:** 3.0X increase (50% → 85% posterior)
- **Outcomes Achievable:** 2.5X increase (70% → 90% posterior)

**Reasoning:** Converges with scientific (+8-12 points, 28% turnover). Only 12% train managers = competitive advantage. Real-world validation.

### Evidence Type 3: ORGANIZATIONAL (FEVS 2024 Benchmark Data)

**Evidence Strength:** MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Sample:** 600,000+ federal employees
- **Quality:** Professional OPM methodology, 20+ year track record
- **10 Barriers Applied:** 1 HIGH barrier (interpretation bias), 4 MODERATE barriers

**Key Findings:**
- FEVS average = 67.3%, mid-size = 66.2% (our 62% is 4-5 points BELOW)
- Lowest areas: Training (58-62%), Growth (64%) - **ALIGN with our M interventions**
- Turnover intent 18.4% matches our 18% actual (validates Y metric)
- 70% target matches supervisor engagement (70-73%) - ambitious but realistic
- 5-7 point manager-staff gap validates manager training focus

**How Much More Confident Are We Now?**
- **The problem is real:** Went from 85% to 95% sure
- **We're fixing the right things:** Went from 70% to 90% sure
- **Our 70% goal makes sense:** Went from 60% to 75% sure

**Why It Matters:** Proves problem is real (62% is 4-5 points below average). Lowest scores in training/growth = exactly what we're fixing.
**Limitation:** Government vs private sector, correlation not causation.

**How Much More Confident Are We Now?**

- **Main risk identified:** Managers might not have the skills yet

**Why It Matters:** All stakeholders benefit. **Risks:** Perceived inauthenticity, manager time constraints.
